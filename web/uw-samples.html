Load your UW samples

Install your firmware first. Then use the sample downloads shown at the end of your Kitbasher build.

  1. Back up the samples you want to keep. These files replace the UW slots listed beside each download. Kit and pattern backups do not include samples.
  2. Connect both MIDI cables. Interface OUT ? Machinedrum IN. Machinedrum OUT ? interface IN.
  3. Prepare the Machinedrum. Open GLOBAL ? FILE ? SAMPLE MGR ? RECEIVE. Choose ORG, then press YES. This uses the destinations saved in the files.
  4. Send with a sample utility. In C6 on Windows, select your MIDI input and output and enable SDS Handshake. Load one downloaded .syx and click Send. Wait for receiving and loading to finish before sending the next file.
  5. Check the sample name and slot. Keep the files unchanged: do not trim, normalize or convert them. Test wavetables through their model; they may sound like noise on their own.

Kitbasher provides the files and instructions; browser sending currently handles firmware only. Elektron Transfer does not send Machinedrum UW samples. On systems that cannot run C6, use a sample sender with MIDI SDS handshake support.

More help

For a complete sample backup, use SAMPLE MGR ? SEND ? ALL with a sample receiver on your computer. See the Machinedrum sample manager instructions.
